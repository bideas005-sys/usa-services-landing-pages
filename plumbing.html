Quick Tip Before the Plumber Arrives

If water is actively spraying or pooling indoors, shut off your home's main water valve right away (usually near the front perimeter, basement, or water meter) and keep electrical cords or appliances away from wet areas.

Frequently Asked Questions

What residential plumbing issues can I call about?

You can call for burst or leaking pipes, clogged kitchen or bathroom drains, main sewer line backups, overflowing toilets, slab leaks, and tank or tankless water heater repairs and replacements.

How much does a typical residential plumbing repair cost in the U.S.?

Standard drain clearing, faucet or toilet repairs, and minor pipe leak fixes commonly range from $150 to $450, while water heater replacements or main sewer line repairs typically range from $900 to $2,800+.

Are urgent plumbing dispatch options available?

Yes. Calls placed during active service windows are routed automatically to available local plumbing providers covering your ZIP code.

What should I do while waiting for a plumber during an active water leak?

Locate and turn off your home's main water shut-off valve immediately, turn off electricity near standing water if safe to do so, and move valuables away from wet floors.
