Important Electrical Safety Warning

If you smell burning plastic or electrical insulation, see sparks or smoke from an outlet or panel, or notice water leaking onto live electrical equipment, do not touch the panel. Evacuate to a safe distance and contact 911 or your local electric utility immediately.

Frequently Asked Questions

What residential electrical problems can I call about?

You can call for repeatedly tripping circuit breakers, dead outlets, faulty GFCI receptacles, flickering lights, electrical panel inspections, ceiling fan installations, and EV charger circuit setups.

How much does a typical residential electrician repair cost?

Minor outlet, switch, or breaker replacements commonly range from $150 to $450, while electrical panel upgrades or dedicated 240V circuit installations typically range from $800 to $2,500+ depending on home wiring and local permits.

How quickly will I be connected to an electrical service provider?

Calls placed during active service windows are routed automatically to available local electrical providers or dispatch partners in your area.

When should I call emergency utility services instead of an electrician?

If you see downed power lines, smell strong burning plastic near a main panel, witness active sparks or electrical fire, or have floodwater touching live wiring, evacuate safely and call 911 or your electric utility immediately.
