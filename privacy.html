Privacy Policy

How visitor information, call-routing metadata, and third-party service connections are handled on USA Home Services Dispatches.

1. Information We Collect

USA Home Services Dispatches collects standard technical browsing information such as IP address, browser type, device category, referring URLs, and page interaction logs to maintain site security, prevent automated abuse, and improve page performance.

When you voluntarily place a telephone call to a dispatch number displayed on this website, call-tracking systems may process call metadata including your caller ID phone number, call timestamp, call duration, geographic area code/ZIP routing data, and call recordings for quality assurance and routing verification where permitted by law.

2. How Information Is Used

We use website analytics and call-routing data strictly to:

• Connect homeowners with available independent residential plumbing, HVAC, electrical, or roofing service providers.
• Evaluate campaign routing accuracy, service availability, and call quality.
• Detect and block fraudulent, spam, or invalid traffic.

3. Sharing With Service Providers & Call Partners

When you initiate a call through a listed dispatch number, your phone number and call connection are routed to participating third-party service providers, contractor networks, or call-routing partners covering your service area.

We do not sell your personal browsing logs to unrelated data brokers. Once your call connects to an independent service provider, that provider's own privacy policy and terms govern any estimates, scheduling, or work performed.

4. Cookies, Analytics & U.S. State Privacy Rights

This site uses standard cookies and Google Analytics (`gtag.js`) to understand aggregate visitor traffic and page load performance. You may disable or clear cookies anytime through your browser settings.

Residents of California and other U.S. states with applicable consumer privacy laws may request information regarding personal data associated with their direct inquiries or request deletion of such records where legally applicable.

5. Contact & Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically as we add new home service categories or update our domain infrastructure.

For privacy inquiries, data requests, or compliance questions, contact us at bideas005@gmail.com.
