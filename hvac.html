Gas & Carbon Monoxide Safety Notice

If you smell rotten eggs (natural gas) near your furnace or your carbon monoxide detector alarms, do not turn lights or appliances on or off. Evacuate everyone from the home immediately and call 911 or your local gas utility emergency number from outside.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do participating HVAC providers service both central air conditioners and furnaces?

Yes. Participating local HVAC providers handle central air conditioners, gas and electric furnaces, heat pumps, thermostats, and airflow issues depending on active service availability in your area.

How much does a typical AC or furnace repair cost in the U.S.?

Common HVAC repairs such as capacitor replacements, thermostat fixes, or ignitor replacements typically range from $150 to $500, while refrigerant leak repairs or blower motor replacements often range from $450 to $1,200+.

Can I call for a seasonal tune-up or a system replacement quote?

Yes. Homeowners can call to request seasonal AC or heating inspections, filter and coil maintenance, or replacement estimates for aging HVAC units.

What should I do if I smell gas near my furnace?

If you smell natural gas or suspect a carbon monoxide leak, leave the home immediately without operating electrical switches, and call 911 or your local gas utility emergency line from outside.
