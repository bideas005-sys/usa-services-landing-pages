Frequently Asked Questions

How does USA Home Services Dispatches work?

Select your residential service category, review common issue coverage and cost ranges, and call the listed dispatch line during active service hours to be routed to an available local service provider.

Does USA Home Services Dispatches perform repairs directly?

No. USA Home Services Dispatches is a free consumer referral and call-connection directory. All inspections, estimates, and repair services are performed by independent third-party providers.

Is there a fee to call a dispatch number on this site?

Connecting through our listed dispatch numbers is free for homeowners. Standard carrier call rates apply, and any service or diagnostic fees are quoted directly by the responding contractor.

Disclosure: USA Home Services Dispatches provides informational guides and call-connection routing to independent third-party service providers and call partners. We do not directly perform on-site repair or contractor work. Availability, licensing, and pricing depend on the responding provider.
